# UPI vs Bank Statement Reconciler

🔗 **Open the app:** https://estherlijo22-hub.github.io/UPI-vs-bank-statement-reconciler/

A browser-based tool that compares your bank statement with your UPI app history to flag unrecognised, mismatched, duplicate, or missing transactions.
All processing happens locally on your device, so no financial data is ever uploaded or stored.

## How to use
1. Download your bank statement as CSV.
2. Download your UPI transaction history as CSV.
3. Upload or paste both files, check the detected columns, and click **Analyse**.
4. Review the flagged transactions and verify them with your bank.

## Note
A flag means "please verify", not proof of fraud. If you find a real unauthorised transaction, report it to your bank and call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
